# Automatic seasonal colors

The owner's requested four-season palette applies to all seven pages. The business uses America/New_York time, regardless of the visitor's location.

- December–February: winter, navy and icy blue.
- March–May: spring, green and light leaf tones.
- June–August: summer, teal and sunshine yellow.
- September–November: fall, copper, brown and amber.

The script runs in the head to select colors before the page appears. It checks again on page restoration, when a tab becomes visible and hourly while open. It does not reload pages, clear forms or store visitor information. If JavaScript is disabled, the default palette is fall.

Color tokens are in seasons.css; calendar selection is in seasons.js. Photos, videos and service copy are not switched by this color feature.
